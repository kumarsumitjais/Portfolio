## Modelling

AQI is a continuous number, so this is a **regression** problem. Three models were trained and compared, from simple to ensemble:

- **Linear Regression**, the baseline. Easy to interpret, but air quality isn't linear.
- **Decision Tree Regressor** captures non-linear interactions, but overfits on 31 days of data.
- **Random Forest Regressor** averages many trees, which cuts overfitting and generalises best.

</Md>

<Cell n={5} code={`X = reg.drop(columns=["DATE", "AQI"]).values
y = reg["AQI"].values

models = {
    "Linear Regression": LinearRegression(),
    "Decision Tree": DecisionTreeRegressor(),
    "Random Forest": RandomForestRegressor(),
}
for name, model in models.items():
    model.fit(X, y)`} />

<Md>

**Result:** Random Forest came out on top with **94% prediction accuracy**, a clear jump over the linear baseline.
